Understanding Oscillations

This is a closer look at the idea underneath the stimulation work on my Current Work page. The tACS approach rests on a specific claim about how a weak oscillating current can engage a brain rhythm that is already there, and that claim is much easier to feel by playing with it than by reading equations.

The module below builds up in six short chapters: from how simple waves combine, through what it actually means for stimulation to capture a rhythm rather than just add to it, to why a single fixed frequency helps some people more than others. Each chapter has a small interactive and a checkpoint to clear before the next one unlocks, or you can skip ahead at any point.

This is a teaching tool and a work in progress; I am adding empirical support and references as it develops.
